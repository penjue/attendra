# Attendra Product Charter

## Purpose
Provide organisations with a reliable way to know who is working, where they reported, when they arrived and left, and how many hours were worked.

## Initial markets
United Kingdom first, Kenya second. Country-specific defaults are configuration, not forks.

## Core users
Platform super admin, company admin/HR, branch manager, employee.

## Product principles
1. Compliance by design.
2. Security by default.
3. Simplicity for frontline staff.
4. Configurable rather than hard-coded.
5. Multi-tenant isolation.
6. Offline resilience.
7. Auditability and no silent attendance edits.
8. Backward-compatible APIs and deployable releases.

## v0.1 success criterion
A registered employee checks in on a branch tablet and the headquarters dashboard shows the attendance event.
